Atlas Manufacturing — Maintenance Policy
========================================

Severity response expectations

Critical:
immediate shutdown and technician response within 15 minutes.

High:
response within 30 minutes.

Medium:
response within 4 hours.

Low:
schedule during next maintenance window.

Hydraulic pressure-loss incidents require inspection before restart.
Safety interlocks may never be bypassed for production.

Any asset with an active critical or high-severity fault must remain locked out
until a qualified technician clears the fault and records verification.
Production supervisors may request temporary workarounds only when documented
risk assessment is completed and signed.

Open maintenance tickets must reference the asset code, active error code when
present, and the observed symptom. Closed tickets must include the corrective
action and restart authorization name.
