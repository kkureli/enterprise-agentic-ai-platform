Atlas Manufacturing — Preventive Maintenance Plan
=================================================

Hydraulic Press
- pressure inspection weekly
- hydraulic oil check weekly
- filter inspection monthly
- full preventive maintenance every 500 operating hours

CNC
- coolant check daily
- spindle inspection monthly
- calibration quarterly

Robotic cells
- visual inspection weekly
- joint/encoder inspection monthly

Assembly machines
- fastener torque audit monthly
- lubrication points service every 250 operating hours

Stamping presses
- die safety check before each production run
- clutch and brake inspection monthly
