Atlas Manufacturing — Safety and Restart Procedure
==================================================

Lockout/tagout requirements
1. Isolate energy sources for the affected asset.
2. Apply personal lock and tag identifying the technician.
3. Verify zero energy state before beginning work.
4. Multiple technicians must apply individual locks when shared work occurs.

Restart authorization
1. Corrective work and verification must be recorded.
2. Active error codes must be cleared or explained.
3. Area supervisor or maintenance lead must authorize restart.
4. Operators may not self-authorize restart after critical or high faults.

Post-maintenance verification
1. Confirm sensors and interlocks function correctly.
2. Run a controlled dry cycle without product when applicable.
3. Confirm no abnormal pressure, temperature, torque, or noise readings.

Operator handoff
1. Brief the operator on work completed and remaining restrictions.
2. Confirm the asset status in the operations system.
3. Release the cell only after acknowledgment of the handoff.
