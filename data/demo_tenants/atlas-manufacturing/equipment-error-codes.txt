Atlas Manufacturing — Equipment Error Codes
==========================================

This document defines tenant-specific equipment fault codes for Atlas
Manufacturing production assets. Always resolve the active code before
returning equipment to production.

E-100
Lubrication pressure below safe operating threshold.
Inspect lubrication pump, oil level, pressure sensor, and supply lines.
Equipment must not resume production until pressure is stable.

AX-4317
Hydraulic pressure loss.
Inspect hydraulic circuit, pressure sensor, reservoir level, and visible leaks.
Restart requires technician verification.

AX-2204
CNC spindle temperature above operating threshold.
Stop machining cycle and inspect coolant flow and spindle lubrication.

RB-2201
Robot joint torque deviation.
Place robot cell in maintenance mode and inspect joint drive and encoder.

AX-1180
Press cycle timeout.
Verify press stroke sensors, interlock state, and operator start sequence.

RB-0904
Weld torch gas flow low.
Inspect shielding gas supply, regulators, and torch hose connections.
