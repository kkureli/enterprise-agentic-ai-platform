Helios Energy Services — Field Error Codes
==========================================

Tenant-specific field equipment fault codes for Helios renewable sites.

E-100
Power inverter communication timeout with site controller.
Check inverter network connection, controller availability, and communication
gateway status.

WT-302
Wind turbine yaw motor overload.
Stop automatic yaw operation and inspect motor load, gearbox resistance, and
yaw drive.

WT-118
Gearbox oil temperature above operating limit.
Reduce turbine load and inspect lubrication system and cooling circuit.

INV-604
DC bus overvoltage.
Place inverter in safe state and inspect DC input and grid conditions.

WT-221
Blade pitch actuator fault.
Suspend automatic pitch control and inspect actuator hydraulics or electrics.

INV-310
Grid frequency out of operating band.
Confirm utility conditions before restoring inverter export.
