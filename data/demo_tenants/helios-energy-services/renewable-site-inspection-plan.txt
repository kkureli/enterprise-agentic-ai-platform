Helios Energy Services — Renewable Site Inspection Plan
=======================================================

Wind turbine monthly inspection
- nacelle visual inspection
- yaw system function check
- safety system verification

Gearbox oil inspection
- oil level and condition check monthly
- oil sampling on the planned quarterly interval

Blade visual checks
- leading-edge and tip condition from ground or drone survey
- lightning receptor inspection when accessible

Inverter checks
- enclosure temperature and ventilation
- DC and AC connection torque audit quarterly
- communications health with the site controller

Transformer inspection
- thermal scan quarterly
- oil level and bushing visual check monthly

Communications health checks
- gateway connectivity daily remote check
- failover path verification monthly
