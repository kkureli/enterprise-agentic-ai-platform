Helios Energy Services — Lockout/Tagout Field Procedure
=======================================================

Electrical isolation
1. Open and lock the turbine or inverter disconnect.
2. Verify absence of voltage with an approved tester.
3. Apply personal lock and tag at the isolation point.

Mechanical isolation
1. Immobilize yaw, pitch, and rotating assemblies as required.
2. Secure access hatches and confirm rotor is in a safe state.
3. Apply mechanical locks where available.

Verification before work
1. Confirm isolation with a second qualified person when required.
2. Review active fault codes and work permit scope.
3. Do not begin work until zero-energy verification is complete.

Restart authorization
1. Remove tools and restore guards.
2. Clear or document remaining faults.
3. Site lead authorizes restart after communications and SCADA health checks.
4. Record restart time and authorizing technician.
