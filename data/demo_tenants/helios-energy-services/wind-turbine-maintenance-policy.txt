Helios Energy Services — Wind Turbine Maintenance Policy
========================================================

Severity classifications
Critical: immediate remote shutdown and field dispatch within 2 hours when safe.
High: field response within 8 hours.
Medium: schedule within 48 hours.
Low: include in the next planned site visit.

Remote inspection rules
- review SCADA trends and active fault codes before dispatch
- attempt safe remote reset only when the fault class allows it
- document remote findings in the maintenance ticket

Field technician escalation
- any yaw, gearbox, or blade pitch critical fault requires field escalation
- electrical isolation must be confirmed before nacelle or inverter work

Shutdown conditions
- gearbox oil over-temperature
- uncontrolled yaw overload
- inverter unsafe DC bus state
- loss of site communications that prevents safe monitoring
