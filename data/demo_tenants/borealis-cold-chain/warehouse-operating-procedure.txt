Borealis Cold Chain — Warehouse Operating Procedure
===================================================

Door-open limits
- chilled and frozen zone doors must not remain open beyond the loading cycle
- if zone temperature rises outside policy, stop loading and escalate

Loading dock procedures
- verify dock seal integrity before opening the cold-zone door
- stage product transfer to minimize exposure time
- close dock doors immediately after trailer disconnect

Alarm escalation
- high-severity refrigeration alarms require response within 15 minutes
- unresolved alarms after 30 minutes escalate to the facilities manager

Temperature logging
- automated sensor logs are reviewed each shift
- manual probe checks are required after any E-100 or CL-402 event

Manual inspection rules
- inspect seals, drains, and evaporator icing at the start of each shift
- quarantine product when exposure exceeds approved ranges
