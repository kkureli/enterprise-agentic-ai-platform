Borealis Cold Chain — Cold Chain Policy
=======================================

Frozen storage target:
-18°C or lower

Chilled storage:
2°C to 8°C

Temperature excursion > 15 minutes:
requires incident review.

High-severity refrigeration alarms:
response within 15 minutes.

Products exposed outside approved temperature range must be quarantined until
quality review.

Dock doors should remain open only for active loading or unloading. Continuous
open-door conditions that threaten zone temperature must trigger escalation to
the shift supervisor and refrigeration technician.

All critical refrigeration faults must be logged with asset code, zone, and
observed temperature trend before clearing the alarm.
