Borealis Cold Chain — Refrigeration Maintenance Plan
====================================================

Compressors
- operating pressure inspection weekly
- vibration and oil level check monthly
- full performance inspection quarterly

Condensers
- condenser cleaning monthly
- fan motor and contactor inspection monthly
- airflow obstruction check weekly

Evaporators
- coil and drain pan inspection monthly
- fan operation check weekly
- defrost performance review monthly

Sensors and controls
- temperature sensor calibration quarterly
- controller communication health check monthly

Refrigerant circuit
- leak checks monthly
- suction and discharge pressure trend review weekly
