Borealis Cold Chain — Refrigeration Error Codes
===============================================

Tenant-specific refrigeration and cold-chain fault codes for Borealis sites.

E-100
Evaporator coil temperature sensor communication failure.
Verify sensor connection, controller communication, and sensor calibration.

CL-209
Low refrigerant suction pressure.
Inspect refrigerant circuit for leaks and verify compressor operating state.

CL-511
Condenser fan failure.
Inspect fan motor, power supply, contactor, and airflow obstruction.

CL-730
Loading dock thermal seal breach.
Inspect dock door seals and ensure cold-zone exposure is minimized.

CL-118
Defrost cycle incomplete.
Inspect heaters, drain path, and defrost termination sensors.

CL-402
Chilled zone high temperature alarm.
Verify setpoint, door state, and evaporator fan operation.
